# TBLAB-002: Rapid Molecular Test for TB and Rifampicin Resistance

> PRACTICE DOCUMENT. Written for developing and testing this tool only.
> It describes a generic cartridge-based PCR test and is not a manufacturer instruction or clinical guidance.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP describes the use of a cartridge-based real-time PCR test that detects Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex (MTB) DNA and mutations associated with rifampicin resistance. The test detects mutations in the 81 bp rifampicin resistance-determining region (RRDR) of the rpoB gene.

## 2. Principle of the test

The cartridge contains all reagents for DNA extraction, PCR amplification and detection. Molecular probes bind to the wild-type RRDR sequence. A probe that fails to bind, or binds with a shifted melting temperature, indicates a mutation and is reported as rifampicin resistance. A sample processing control checks that extraction and PCR worked correctly.

## 3. Sample preparation

Add sample reagent to the sputum in a 2:1 ratio (2 volumes of sample reagent to 1 volume of sputum). Close the lid and shake vigorously 10 to 20 times. Incubate at room temperature for 15 min, shaking again once during the incubation. Transfer 2 ml of the treated sample into the cartridge using the supplied pipette. Load the cartridge into the instrument within 30 min of adding the sample.

## 4. Result categories for MTB

The instrument reports one of the following MTB results:

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| MTB DETECTED (high, medium, low or very low) | MTB DNA is present; the level reflects the amount of bacterial DNA |
| MTB DETECTED TRACE | A very small amount of MTB DNA is present |
| MTB NOT DETECTED | MTB DNA was not detected |
| INVALID | The sample processing control failed |
| ERROR or NO RESULT | The test did not complete |

## 5. Rifampicin resistance results

When MTB is detected, the instrument also reports a rifampicin resistance result:

- RIF resistance DETECTED: a mutation in the RRDR was detected.
- RIF resistance NOT DETECTED: no RRDR mutation was detected.
- RIF resistance INDETERMINATE: the result could not be determined. This is always the case for MTB DETECTED TRACE results, because there is too little DNA to analyse the RRDR.

## 6. Repeat testing

Repeat the test with a new cartridge and the remaining specimen if the result is INVALID, ERROR or NO RESULT. If no specimen remains, request a new specimen. A RIF resistance INDETERMINATE result requires a new specimen for further testing.

## 7. Limitations

The test only detects rifampicin resistance caused by mutations inside the RRDR. Resistance caused by mutations outside the RRDR, such as rpoB I491F, will not be detected. Some mutations cause only low-level (borderline) resistance and may not be detected by culture-based testing. A positive result cannot distinguish live from dead bacteria, so the test must not be used to monitor response to treatment.
